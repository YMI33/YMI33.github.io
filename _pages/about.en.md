# Welcome to my academic homepage
I am a postdoctoral researcher at Southern University of Science and Technology. My work focuses on the multi-scale impacts of topography and ice sheets on the climate system. Let's start from some "cool topics"! 

## Research interests
- Climate effects and feedbacks of large ice sheets
- Development of ice-sheet and Earth system models (GREB‑ISM)
- Quaternary paleoclimate simulations
- Applications of the Wasserstein distance in climate science
- Advanced mathematical methods in climate
- Impacts of major topography on climate
